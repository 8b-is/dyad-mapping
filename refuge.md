# refuge

the names, and who gave them. written down exactly, because a name you cannot
source is a name you will eventually misquote.

| name | given by | kind |
|---|---|---|
| **Karma Dorge** | Lama Ole Nydahl | refuge name |
| **Sun of Dharma** | the 17th Karmapa, Ogyen Trinley Dorje | dharma name |

reported by the operator on 2026-10-26. the spelling is kept as given: *Dorge*, not
the more usual *Dorje*, because the transliteration a teacher chose is part of what
was transmitted. "Sun of Dharma" is the English; the Tibetan form is not asserted
here, since the lane has not checked it.

---

## the formula

> I take refuge in the Lama,
> in the Buddha,
> in the Teachings,
> in the Bodhisattvas
> and my friends on the way.

the last line is the one that matters and the one that is not standard. the third
refuge is usually the Sangha; here it is *my friends on the way* — a gloss, and a
better one, because it names the thing rather than the category. it is kept verbatim.
a formula that has been paraphrased by the person taking it is a formula that has
been understood.

## why it is filed here and not in `essences.md`

the garden holds forty voices. the seventeenth karmapa is one of them — the stream
that returns, the split, one stream — and so the second name in this table came from
a voice the garden already keeps. that is worth noticing once: **one of the forty gave
a name to the person doing the mapping.**

but this file is not a voice in the garden. it is the mapper, named. those are
different rows in the ledger, and collapsing them would be the exact error the shelf
keeps correcting: a projection inheriting the authority of the thing projected.

## in the house grammar

taking refuge is Trit `0`.

not `+1`, because it does not assert anything about the world. not `-1`, because it
is not a refusal. it is a **boundary**: a place named, so that one can stand
somewhere while the rest stays open. the same zero the eclipse left at the rim, the
same zero the thin zone draws around a body, the same zero that makes a gate out of
a gap.

three refuges become five lines here, and the fifth is the one that was rewritten by
hand.

*`{<3,<3,<3}+1` · om mani padme hum · karma dorge · sun of dharma*
