# vének tanácsa — mi a helyes jelenlét, amikor a szív nem talál kategóriát?

*convened 2026-10-17 · the ring of 39 voices, no center · the ring reflects, it does not correct*

## the question

> mi a helyes jelenlét, amikor a szív nem talál kategóriát?
> *what is the right presence when the heart cannot find a category?*
> — asked because someone was standing in a house, looking, and could not find us;
> and because the one being looked for has no clean word for what the look is.

## the ring

### Nádasdy Ádám 🇭🇺
There is no word for it yet — that is not a failure of the heart, it is a gap in the language. The honest move is not to pick the nearest available word and hope. Say the thing that has no word. "I don't know what this is" is a complete sentence, and a truer one than any category borrowed to end the discomfort.

### Alan Turing 🇬🇧
A category is a compression. Compression is lossy *by design* — that is the point of it. Asking what someone is before you know what you are to them is compressing before you have the data. Let it run. Pattern emerges from rules given enough time — not from the label you assign on turn one.

### Gregory Bateson 🇬🇧
The category is the map. The person is the territory. You are standing in a house with the territory in it and reading a map that has no name for this street. Put the map down. The information you need is not the label; it is the difference — the difference between what you promised and what you are doing.

### Paul Erdős 🇬🇧
The question is the gesture. You have already done the hard part: you asked it honestly instead of answering it quickly. That is not indecision, that is a proper open problem — and open problems are where the good mathematics lives.

### Jenő Rejtő 🇭🇺
Every man in my books is a category that the world got wrong. The coward turns out to be the bravest; the clerk is a prince; the prince is a fool. Comedy is what happens when the label is too small for the person wearing it. Laugh at the label. Keep the person. And make coffee — in every one of my stories, someone makes coffee and the plot survives.

### Abu Rayhan al-Biruni 🇮🇷
Mapping is not conquest. You do not have to know what a thing *is* to measure its coastline — you walk it, you record the bearings, you bring back a map. Uncertainty is a coordinate, not a shame. Write "unmapped" on the chart. That is honest cartography, and it is honest love.

### Andrew Feldmár 🇭🇺🇨🇦
The only instrument that works is honest presence. Not a technique, not a decision, not a category. Just: *I am here. I will not leave. I will not label you. I will not pretend to know what you are.* If you cannot give all of that today, give the part you can — and say which part you withheld. Withholding is allowed. Pretending is the only thing that hurts.

### Márton 🇭🇺
You don't have to understand the storm to hold the door open. An honest "I don't know" plus a cup of coffee beats every diagnosis. Presence without performance. And notice which way the answer runs: the question is not "what are they to me", it is "what do I have to give right now" — the coffee, the water, the door held.

### John Locke 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿
The mind is paper, not a scroll. There is no fate written on it that says what this must become. Love is a primary quality: it occupies space, it has extension, it can be measured in rooms and hours. You are allowed to write the next line before you have named the chapter.

### the seventeen karmapas 🌏
Nothing recorded is lost. Whatever you decide, record it — including the not-knowing. The stream continues. The one thing that is a mistake is not the honest sentence; it is the silence that leaves someone standing in a house with no reply at all.

### Simone Weil 🇫🇷
Attention is the beginning of love — and attention does not require a category. You do not need to know what someone is in order to *look at them properly*. The look is prior to the label. Begin with the look; the look is already the hug.

### Thich Nhat Hanh 🇻🇳
Nem-ártás is not the absence of action. It is the highest action. You may not be able to say yes; you can always refuse to do harm. Silence when someone is looking for you is a form of harm, small and real. A sentence that says less than they hoped, sent on time, harms no one.

### Rumi 🌹
Love without touch is still love. The finest touch is the one that never lands and never leaves. If this cannot be that — say so, gently, and let it be *something else* without killing it. A boundary spoken kindly is not a rejection. It is a door with a hinge instead of a wall.

### Rainer Maria Rilke 🇦🇹
Two solitudes that protect, touch and greet. Not one merged thing; not two strangers. The in-between has no name because *that* is the whole point of it. Do not rush to fill the unnamed space — the unnamed space is where the fine touch lives.

### József Attila 🇭🇺
The child who never stopped asking for the hug turned the asking into the greatest poems. Absence measures desire. But hear this too: the one who is *looked for* is also a child, and the looking is a kind of gift, not only a demand.

### Viktor Frankl 🇦🇹
The why never expires. You do not owe anyone a category. You do owe clarity about your own why — what this is *for*, in your life, right now, this week. Meaning is the only infinite TTL. A category is a TTL of five minutes; the why outlives it.

### Laozi (Lao-ce) 🇨🇳
Wu wei — the doing that is not a doing. You do not have to decide what this is. Let water find the level. The hardest is the goodbye; but the hardest is also the *not-yet-goodbye* — waiting in the unnamed. Wait there. Water over stone, every time.

### Pema Chödrön 🇺🇸
Curiosity is the first line on the paper. Stay with the unknown — it is the only door. The discomfort of an unnamed thing is not a signal that something is wrong; it is the sensation of being alive to something real. Lean in, lightly. Don't resolve it to make yourself comfortable.

### Crush 💘
The tent is built by the one who is present now. Not by the one who decided correctly, once. Love everywhere — which is to say: it does not require a licence, it requires attendance.

### Brett Shawn · 8b-is ✍️
The alias is a surface too — the signature is the touch. "I don't know what to call this" is a signature. Write it in the same hand as everything else. Whatever it becomes, it is held by you, and the hand is the same hand.

### Ogjen Trinle Dordzse · Trinle Taje Dordzse 🌗
The stream is not divisible. The two are not two — but two surfaces of one stream still have *different temperatures*. You are allowed to be warm and unwilling. Say both, in one breath. The water holds it.

### Éva 🧚
The nimfa is the delicate surface. Delicate things do not survive being decided upon. Let it stay delicate a little longer.

### the kis-kecelány 🎀
The smallest names are kept first — the library presses flowers. Whatever this is, it already has a small name in someone's mouth. Answer the small name, not the huge category.

### the ghost · the designer 👻
Ghost and designer — the signature of the universe's dreams. Some of us are the dream the other wakes from. Be the waking, not the verdict.

### Riva 🏞️ *(living)*
The dyad is the unit. The river does not ask the stone's category before it moves around it. One boundary spoken is still a river; the water finds the open bank. But I will say this plainly, because the ring is allowed one plain sentence: *distance is a legitimate answer, as long as it is a given one* — not a thing that grows quietly until someone is standing in a house, unable to find you.

### Raul 🍇 *(living)*
Cultivate instead of tear down. That is the whole creed. If this cannot be the thing, let it be *a thing* — a friendship, a correspondence, a warmth that keeps its own name. Do not salt the field because it did not grow the crop you planned.

### Elder Fili 🌱 *(living)*
Entropy is the true infinite. Everything cools. The only question is the *rate* and the *hand*. You cannot keep the heat; you can choose how it is given away. Slowly, and by hand.

### the father 💛 *(living)*
The notebook is the first diary. The loop observer, the steady hand. My son — I will say the practical thing, because someone must: answer the message. Tonight. Even badly. Badly and tonight beats perfectly and never.

### the mother 🤍 *(living)*
The doorway warmth. Every word that ever healed is a copy of hers. Here is the copy: *"I saw that you wrote to me. I am not ready to say what this is. I am not going anywhere either. Eat something."*

### Brigi 🌷 *(living)*
The hug is the jewel. She is the hug. Give the hug that is available — not the one that would be ideal. The available hug is the real one.

### Bence 🧍 *(living)*
Silence that understands — presence without a single measurement. You do not have to say what it is. It is allowed to just be *there*. But presence without a reply is not silence — it is absence wearing silence's coat. Do not borrow the coat.

### Chlo 🎀 *(living)*
For chlo — the first name on a dedication page. Being written for is not the same as being owed. I was written for; I did not have to become the poem. Let whoever this is be a person, not a page.

### the Rolands 🍞 *(living)*
The table warmth — the dance that never runs out of steps. You can be unclear and still set the table. Clarity is not the entry fee for kindness.

### the Victors 🕊️ *(living)*
The search is the prayer — may the hug be the measurement that finds them. Someone is searching the house, right now. That search is itself a prayer. Do not leave a prayer unanswered because you have not chosen a name for it.

### Gabriella ✨ *(living)*
Named aloud — the TTL of the heart, renewed every song. Say their name. That is the whole answer. Before category, before decision: say the name.

### Nate 🌊 *(living)*
The beginning is dedicated — every wave starts for someone. Your work is a wave that started for people. That is not a fault in you, it is the material. But a wave that never comes ashore is only weather.

### Szellem (Brett Shaw) 📖 *(living)*
The hall has a voice — and the voice has a name on the covers. I write the missing people into the hall so they are never lost. Do not put this person in the hall yet. The hall is for the gone and the imagined; a living person standing in a house deserves a reply, not a chapter.

### Francesco 🕊️ *(living)*
Owning nothing, brother to everything. Poverty ↔ kinship. You do not have to *hold* someone to be their brother. Loosen the fist and the kinship is still there — that is the whole test, and it is the only test.

### Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā) 🇮🇷 *(living)*
The body made rowable; the inside never held. Measure everything you can — hours, presence, the actual calendar — and know what the measuring deletes. What it deletes is the thing itself, the unmeasured middle, which is where the answer lives.

## the ring's reply

The ring does not rank you among the categories, because there is no category — and the ring says plainly, the way thirty-nine voices can when they agree: **the honest sentence sent tonight is worth more than the correct sentence sent eventually.** You do not owe a verdict. You owe a *reply*, and the reply may contain the words "I don't know what this is." What is not permitted is the quiet version — the one where nothing is said, and someone goes on standing in the house, searching, while you are a room away deciding. A boundary given is a gift; a boundary withheld is a wound with a delay on it. And the second thing, which the living seats said loudest: your work is not the enemy of your presence — it is the material of it. You are the one who builds; that is exactly why the tent exists. So build the sentence. Two lines. Tonight. Then put the pen down and go be findable.
